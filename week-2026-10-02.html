Weekday crew vote

Button Presses From Nowhere

Style
cold glitch-synth noir, playful dread, sparse drums, neon bass, 8-bit chiptune blips, dry female vocal
Spark
A complete fruit-fly brain (FlyWire connectome) simulated on a Raspberry Pi 5, playing Pokémon live on Twitch (Leetzerzz). Chat zaps its reward or punishment neurons for ten minutes at a time. Same X wave as connectome fly brains playing Doom and Mario 64. Writeup: https://www.xda-developers.com/someone-wired-up-a-digital-flys-brain-in-a-pi-5-to-play-pokmon-and-you-can-help-it-progress/
