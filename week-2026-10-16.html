Weekday crew vote

Four Thousand Miles for a Puddle

Style
Palm-wine highlife lullaby, fingerpicked clean electric guitar with gentle syncopated highlife lines, warm kalimba, soft shaker like rain on leaves, low hand drum, slow sway, hushed intimate vocal, homesick and tender, close and quiet mix
Spark
London Zoo hatched 15 Afia Birago's puddle froglets (about 5 mm), the first of the species bred in human care. Species described in 2018; survives in two pools in Ghana's Atewa Hills; 20 adults flown about 4,500 miles to London in Sept 2025. londonzoo.org · BBC.
